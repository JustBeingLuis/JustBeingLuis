### Overview

M.Sc. student in Computer Science at **Universidad Industrial de Santander (UIS)**, Colombia. Researcher at **PIGROUP** (Inverse Problems Research Group).

My work focuses on the intersection of **Physics-Informed Deep Learning**, **Computational Imaging**, and **Generative Models**. Specifically, I formulate inverse problem solutions for time-reversed scene reconstruction from transient thermal residues, high dynamic range scene recovery via modulo imaging, and optics-informed neural architectures.

### Research Highlights

* **Time-Reversed Scene Reconstruction (IEEE ICIP)**:
  Formulated a constrained diffusion model conditioned on Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and heat-diffusion physics to invert the thermal dissipation process, recovering past human-object interactions where standard RGB sensor data fails. Outperforms RGB baselines with **PSNR 18.57 dB** and **SSIM 0.79**.

* **Modulo Imaging for Extreme Dynamic Range Sensing**:
  Designed a sensor saturation mitigation framework using modulo optical encoding for drone and autonomous vehicle detection. Eliminates the high computational cost of full HDR reconstruction, sustaining **F1-scores > 96%** under extreme glare.
