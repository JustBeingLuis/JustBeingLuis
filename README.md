## Research

> [!NOTE]
> Currently an undergraduate researcher at **PIGROUP** (Inverse Problems Research Group) — Oct 2024 to present.

<details>
<summary><strong>Drone detection under extreme illumination</strong></summary>

Proposed a fine-tuning methodology using modulo imaging to mitigate sensor saturation. The approach bypasses HDR reconstruction entirely, achieving **F1-scores above 96%** across varying illumination levels with reduced inference time.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Time-reversed scene reconstruction from thermal traces</strong></summary>

Developed a constrained diffusion pipeline guided by Visual-Language Models to infer past interactions from thermal residues. Outperforms RGB-only baselines with **PSNR 18.57 dB** and **SSIM 0.79**.

</details>
